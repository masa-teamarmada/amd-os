## Where theory still leaks too early

The residue is mostly in naming transitions.

Ch15 earns BZM, but the heading `BZMは、現場を置き換えない` and the line "この時点で、会議に初めて BZM という名前を置くことができる" still sound like the narrator is formally announcing the theory. The scene already did the work. The next pass can make the name appear more quietly inside the meeting.

Ch16 repeatedly uses "あとで...と呼ぶ." That phrase is safe in a draft note, but in a book it reminds the reader that a syllabus exists behind the scene. The terms TRL/BRL/GRL/SRL/HRL should arrive as labels the group writes on the map after each contradiction is felt, not as narrator previews.

Ch17 has the same issue with `mu_A`, `mu_G`, `mu_I`, and `sigma_SU`. The email-clock scene is strong enough; the variable notation can be delayed until after the field corridor is fully drawn, or moved into a short methodology note / Field Toolkit side note.

Ch18's FRL section is conceptually correct but too compressed. It introduces FRL, F_character, and F_capability in a definitional block after the repeat-back scene. The reader can feel the theory being poured over the scene rather than discovered inside it.

The hidden-list problem has improved from Act IV, but it remains in Ch18's 90-day role paper and Ch16's closing action sequence. These are useful tools; they read better when they are visibly framed as artifacts to extract, not as main-body prose pretending not to be a checklist.
