## Public-rewrite raw materials

### Score to survival

Do not present Ch19 as a score chapter. The public rewrite should use the source
materials to show four conversions:

- rank -> next uncertainty to reduce;
- high expectation -> survival conversation;
- score threshold -> meeting opener, not automatic judgment;
- P/R_net comparison -> evidence-card question, not formal public model.

The PRS sources are especially useful as a caution. `R_net=low` must not become
`NO_GO` by itself. Some fields can survive through grants, regulatory sequence,
licensing, or other non-sales paths. The useful public question is: "What keeps
the project alive without stealing the resource needed for the main path?"

### Old evidence rule to new evidence rule

Ch20 should borrow from protocol and failure-learning sources. The durable form
is:

| old rule | observed break | new rule | later observation |
|---|---|---|---|
| corporate interest means customer readiness | budget owner absent | split scout / evaluator / budget owner / adoption owner | check at 30/90 days |
| doctor cooperation means field readiness | technician and ethics load appear | split user, operator, ethics, patient explanation | check before PoC |
| experienced executive means founder function rises | protected line is not repeated accurately | test translation trust before title | check in weekly bad-news behavior |
| grant adoption means readiness rose | low axes remain | attach each fund use to one uncertainty | monthly low-axis review |

### ERS as nursery operating design

Ch21 and Ch24 should not frame ERS as "research institution score." The source
material is stronger when treated as operating design:

- `unknown`: confirm within days;
- `not_started`: build within the pilot;
- `drafting`: standardize and stress-test;
- `established`: check whether it reaches real work.

The public rewrite should show institutional capability through changed work:
fewer repeated explanations by researchers, safer disclosure before company
meetings, clearer owner of budget-owner access, and a monthly review where WAIT
does not disappear.

### Field-note safety loop

The Textbook Insight spec and local applier scripts are not public material, but
the method is valuable:

- observe before concluding;
- classify facts, hypotheses, interpretation, unconfirmed, promise-forbidden;
- decide confidentiality before reuse;
- require review before theory/rubric change;
- append only after the source has become a public question or tool.

This is the mechanism that keeps direct author instructions from being erased:
source note -> directive ledger -> insertion map -> rewrite acceptance gate.
