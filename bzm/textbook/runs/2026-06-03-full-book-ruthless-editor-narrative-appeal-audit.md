## Executive verdict

The manuscript is no longer a disguised internal workbook. It now has a real
book spine: one over-strong deck sentence travels through customer evidence,
clock conflict, researcher isolation, disclosure risk, incorporation timing,
WAIT, investor pressure, theory entrance, RESOURCE_SHIFT, evidence-rule repair,
and institution memory.

Cold commercial verdict: promising, unusually useful, not yet a page-turner.

The best chapters read like a trade book about a specific kind of institutional
accident. Ch00, Ch01, Ch05, Ch06, Ch08, Ch09, Ch15, Ch19, Ch20, and Ch21 have
live rooms, documents that change, and emotional cost. Ch19 is currently the
strongest late-book chapter because RESOURCE_SHIFT finally subtracts something
attractive and makes reasonable people unhappy.

The main remaining problem is not company-promo leakage. It is reader fatigue.
Several chapters repeatedly cool the story into correct explanation after the
scene has already taught the point. The book still says "this means..." too
often at the exact moment the reader should be allowed to feel consequence.

This should not trigger another full rewrite. The next pass should be surgical:
demote theory-name rehearsal, compress chapter-end teaching bridges, turn a few
midbook generalizations into sharper consequences, and keep Ch22-Ch24 clearly
as Field Toolkit rather than a second ending.

## What now works

The manuscript now has a central wound. The first over-strong sentence is not a
metaphor; it travels into memos, interview practice, disclosure, investor
questions, and later evidence rules.

The researcher is no longer a caricature. Their silence is credible because the
same support system praises them, displaces them, asks them to disclose, and
expects them to carry company meaning before the conditions are ready.

Supporters, investors, universities, companies, and external executive
candidates are mostly locally rational. The support boundary pass helped. The
book no longer gets its drama by making institutions stupid.

The author directive on survival probability, earning body, premature
incorporation, and J-curve / IPO caution is retained. Ch06 and Ch19 handle it
with actual tradeoff, not sermon.

BZM is introduced later than before and more honestly. Ch15 earns the name by
showing that ordinary field words cannot hold all the contradictions.

The Field Toolkit is now useful. Ch22-Ch24 should not be judged as narrative
chapters; as post-story reader tools, they are coherent.

## What still feels like homework

1. Chapter-end teaching bridges are too frequent.
   - Examples: Ch03 explicitly says `Act I`; Ch06 previews the decision-label
     vocabulary; Ch10, Ch12, Ch14, Ch16, Ch18, and Ch20 end by telling the reader
     what the next conceptual unit will be.
   - Reason: These bridges help workers preserve architecture, but readers feel
     the outline underneath the prose.
   - Fix order: remove or soften only the meta bridge sentences. Keep the
     scene-to-scene pull.

2. Ch16-Ch18 still shift from story to glossary.
   - Examples: `あとでTRLと呼ぶ`, `あとでBRLと呼ぶ`, `あとでGRLと呼ぶ`,
     `あとでSRLと呼ぶ`, `あとでHRLと呼ぶ`, `mu_A`, `mu_G`, `mu_I`,
     `sigma_SU`, `FRL`, `F_character`, `F_capability`.
   - Reason: The repeated "later we name this" device becomes a lecture rhythm.
   - Fix order: keep one theory reveal per chapter, move the rest into a compact
     margin/method note or appendix note, and let the scene carry the axis.

3. Ch11-Ch14 do not always escalate the case.
   - Ch11 has three strong emails, but after that it explains macro pressure more
     than it changes the room.
   - Ch12 is useful but highly diagnostic; it needs one concrete wrong decision
     that almost happens because "prepared" stays singular.
   - Ch14 has good institution language, but parts read like a policy essay
     after the loneliness scene.
   - Fix order: add or sharpen one consequence per chapter, not more theory.

4. Ch13 and Ch18 remain too close emotionally.
   - Ch13 is the external candidate behavior scene.
   - Ch18 is supposed to be theory translation, but it still restages Ch13's
     repeat-back logic.
   - Fix order: do not cut Ch13. Compress Ch18 harder and make it a naming /
     placement chapter, not another candidate test.

5. Ch22-Ch24 are still numbered like the end of the story.
   - The H1 says Field Toolkit, which helps. But in a full-book sequence, a buyer
     may experience them as three extra chapters after the emotional ending.
   - Fix order: package them as Appendix / Field Toolkit A-C in TOC and route
     metadata. Do not expand their prose as narrative.
