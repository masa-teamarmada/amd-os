## §1 Introduction (900w, P1–P7)

- **P1** Hook: University deep-tech projects are screened, funded, and pushed toward incorporation using tools that presuppose a firm — DCF and VC-method valuations, readiness levels, composite scores — yet the object being screened is not a firm. It has no legal entity, no financial statements, no settled founding team, and its failures leave no records.
- **P2** Define Before Zero: the interval from public disclosure of a research result to incorporation. Decisions concentrated in this interval (IP consolidation, team assembly, disclosure sequencing, timing of incorporation) largely determine what the future firm can become; cites: Vohora, Wright & Lockett 2004 (critical junctures); Rasmussen, Mosey & Wright 2011.
- **P3** Flagship: we bring the additionality logic of ex-post program evaluation — value net of displacement, net of what would have happened anyway, socially discounted — to ex-ante, project-level selection in this domain. The move that makes it operable: shift the object of evaluation from the venture itself to a **registered plan** over an observable monthly state. Cites: Link & Scott 2011 (program-level benefit calculation, explicitly built on); David, Hall & Toole 2000 (additionality debate).
- **P4** Genre declaration: design science research — an artifact designed, implemented, and evaluated inside one venture builder's operations, reported with design propositions and falsification conditions rather than claims of external validity. Cites: van Aken 2004; Romme 2003; Gregor & Hevner 2013.
- **P5** ≪限定≫ Scope of "selection": the framework replaces the **objective function** of screening; it does not issue go/no-go decisions or allocate funds. Investment decisions live in a second tier that weighs V against costs, portfolio constraints, and fiduciary duties. V is a gross value measure; resource costs are the second tier's ledger. (1 sentence each, hard commitments.)
- **P6** Three contributions: (i) formalization of the Before Zero measurement problem (five broken premises); (ii) an additionality-based valuation framework whose object is a registered plan — with the operating system that makes it auditable (evidence grades, event registry, gaming resistance); (iii) evidence on **information sufficiency**: project rankings are dominated by information that operational records do not capture.
- **P7** Roadmap paragraph (2 sentences).

## §2 The measurement problem (1,200w, P8–P17)

- **P8** Four absences, stated as measurement preconditions: (1) no legal entity → no boundary for accounts; (2) no financial statements → no cash-flow base; (3) no settled management team → the carrier of execution is itself undetermined; (4) no survivorship record → failures before incorporation vanish (left truncation). Table 1 anchors. Cites: Yang & Aldrich 2012 (left truncation).
- **P9** The fifth broken premise: **running out of money is not death before incorporation.** Absorption-at-zero mortality models presuppose a firm that must meet payroll; a research lab slows down, survives on institutional infrastructure, and waits for the next public grant. Speed changes; the process does not terminate. Consequence: any valuation that treats cash-out as an absorbing state imports a firm-premise into a domain where it does not hold. Cites: Levinthal 1991 (the model whose premise breaks); Vohora et al. 2004.
- **P10** Why firm-valuation tools break: DCF/VC method need cash-flow projections anchored in statements; real options need a definable underlying and exercise cost — before zero, even the cost series is undefined. Cites: Damodaran 2009 (young-company valuation as the nearest attempt); Lerner & Nanda 2020 (VC model's limits for deep tech).
- **P11** Why readiness scales and composite indicators break: TRL is ordinal and single-axis; composite indicators impose weights whose arbitrariness is well documented; both collapse heterogeneous absences into one number. Cites: (composite critique anchors from model ledger's refs; JRC handbook 要確認 at S6).
- **P12** What the additionality tradition supplies — and what it lacks here. Social CBA selects projects ex ante by shadow-priced net social benefit (Little & Mirrlees 1974; Dasgupta, Marglin & Sen 1972 / UNIDO; Squire & van der Tak 1975); ATP applied but-for tests at project level; Link & Scott 2011 codified counterfactual benefit calculation; SROI's forecast mode operationalizes deadweight, displacement, attribution, drop-off (SROI guide 要確認). What none of them supply: a way to define the project when there is no firm, no cost series, no output definition — the object of evaluation itself is missing.
- **P13** Nearest mathematical relative: value-of-information project selection in health technology assessment (EVPI/EVSI integrates expected social value over a parameter prior — same two-stage structure as our V). Difference: HTA selects research questions within a defined trial pipeline; here the pipeline (the plan) is what must be constructed and registered. Cites: Claxton 1999.
- **P14** Stage models and TTO triage: pre-company phases are described (Ndonzuau et al. 2002; Vohora et al. 2004; Clarysse et al. 2005; Rasmussen et al. 2011) and TTOs already triage disclosures by expected value (Jensen & Thursby 2001; Thursby & Thursby 2002; Siegel et al. 2003) — but stages are not mapped to a money-denominated, plan-conditional value. The gap is the mapping, not the phenomenon.
- **P15** Left truncation as a design constraint for validation, not a footnote: firm registries only see survivors of Before Zero; prospective case registration from first contact is the only unbiased sampling frame. Cites: Yang & Aldrich 2012.
- **P16** ≪限定≫ Which counterfactual: we measure **enterprise-level additionality** (value net of what others would have realized without this project), not **investor additionality** (what changes because this builder/fund engages). The two coincide only under an assumption we state and register for falsification. Cites: Brest & Born 2013.
- **P17** Gap statement: no existing apparatus defines a valuation object for a firm-less project, prices its industrial contribution net of displacement and counterfactual realization, and remains auditable under gaming pressure. That is the artifact this paper reports.

## §3 Design propositions (900w, P18–P24)

- **P18** Derivation procedure, disclosed: propositions distilled from one venture builder's 2026 operating decisions (founder-confirmed requirement set; decision log cited in SM-E); presented as design propositions in the design-science sense — normative, falsifiable through use. Cites: van Aken 2004; Churchill 1979 (content-validity logic, applied qualitatively).
- **P19** DP1–DP6 (Table 2, rows 1–6): conversion capacity; strategic slack (with the fifth-premise corollary — slack changes speed, not survival, before incorporation); self-propulsion (earning capacity independent of external finance); timing window closing from both sides; sector momentum; money-denominated output. Each row: proposition → theoretical footing → what had to be built because the literature starts "after the firm exists."
- **P20** DP7–DP12 (Table 2, rows 7–12): absorb all field conditions that move score precision; exit-form independence; carrier as a bundle of functions (vacancy = delay, not penalty); ceiling (the industry an application set can create); unit economics as a hard gate; capital intensity. Same three-column treatment. Cites per row: Penrose 1959; Teece 2007; Bourgeois 1981; Levinthal 1991; Winborg & Landström 2001; Myers & Majluf 1984; McDonald & Siegel 1986; Weeds 2002; Etzkowitz & Leydesdorff 2000; Morgeson, DeRue & Karam 2010; Wasserman 2003; Lazear 2005; Shane & Stuart 2002; Beckman et al. 2007; Teece 1986; Wright 1936; Gutowski et al. 2009.
- **P21** Table 2 carries an **implementation-status column** ≪開示≫: which propositions are fully implemented in the reference implementation, which are approximated (declared A1–A16), which await calibration. Nothing in the table is hidden behind the text.
- **P22** Rejected candidate requirements (table): inverted-U slack (evidence is from established firms; George 2005 and Vanacker et al. 2013 show positive slope for young private firms); Monte-Carlo trials at judgment-quality inputs; irreversibility points as a separate requirement; IP-ownership as a standalone requirement — each with the rejection reason. This table is the boundary of the requirement set.
- **P23** Non-independence among propositions and the double-counting discipline that follows (slack ⇄ capital intensity; conversion ⇄ slack; learning-curve cost ⇄ cumulative investment). One-effect-one-entry rule forward-referenced to §6.
- **P24** What is deliberately outside: institutional readiness of the host organization, and the causal effect of builder intervention — separate theories; conflating them with project valuation reproduces the composite-indicator failure.

## §4 The framework (1,200w, P25–P33)

- **P25** Object of evaluation: a **registered plan** π^plan over an observable monthly state x_t — not the venture, not the team, not a score of merit. What is scored is "what this plan, run on this state, is expected to create."
- **P26** State x_t, nine components (gate position; free and restricted cash as yen stocks; unresolved rights/approvals; incorporation flag; contract-work lock-in; active applications; success/failure history; review countdown). Math: state tuple + cash transition equations (compact form; full in SM-A).
- **P27** Project parameters θ (13 components: conversion capacity, evangelist-function fill, momentum, process type, technical core, appropriability, willingness-to-pay caps, ceilings, displacement shares, counterfactual schedules, cost floors, self-propulsion). ≪開示≫ Stated as an approximation: "θ is treated as constant over the horizon **because its variation cannot be separately identified**, not because capabilities are static" — tension with the dynamic-capabilities footing acknowledged in two sentences. Cites: Teece 2007; Baker & Nelson 2005; Sirmon et al. 2011.
- **P28** Prior B_0: four-point elicitation (low/best/high/confidence) from source-tagged evidence; correlated components share source variables; structured-expert-judgment lineage. Cites: Cooke 1991; Colson & Cooke 2017; Fairfield & Charman 2017.
- **P29** Plan rules condition **only on observables** (state, funding-window list, contract terms) — never on θ, which no party observes. Registered from a finite template (gate order, failure branching, incorporation condition, offer response, contract-work policy, stop condition); Tier-0 defaults give every project a defined score; deviations from default are displayed. Math: π^plan as a policy on x_t.
- **P30** Relation to effectuation: what is registered is a **policy conditioned on observed events**, not a predictive fixed plan; evidence that explicit decision rules improve termination and pivoting supports rather than contradicts the design (Camuffo et al. 2020; Sarasvathy 2001 acknowledged). Scope condition ≪開示≫: goal transformation through new stakeholders (the core of effectuation) is outside — application sets are fixed within an evaluation version (A16).
- **P31** Monthly transition (order of operations: deadlines → stochastic loss → process draws → rule application → cash update → absorption/self-sufficiency check → history update) and the nine terminal classes measured on one yen scale. Before incorporation, cash-out changes speed, not termination (fifth premise, §2) — 会社化前後の非対称はここで式に接続。⚠数値(dormancy 改訂の承認状態を凍結時に反映).
- **P32** Score: V = ∫ E[Π|θ, π^plan] dB_0(θ). Two-stage uncertainty separated by what information can do: scenario branching (irreducible) vs. parameter ignorance (reducible — and the model reports **which** parameter to investigate next). Math: display equation + one-line reading.
- **P33** Reporting format: 10/50/90 percentiles of v(θ); upper÷lower as "how much is not yet known"; value-bearing path probabilities; continuation-value share flagged when majority. Grid computation, no Monte Carlo at judgment-quality inputs; effective two digits.

## §5 Valuing industrial creation (1,300w, P34–P43)

- **P34** Π(ω): for each month, domestic value added created by **active applications** (entry at adoption/production-contract gate; ramp-up; two exit channels — event-driven and obsolescence-hazard), discounted at the social rate; terminal continuation value. Math: Π display equation; components table. Cites: Miller & Blair 2022 (value-added definition).
- **P35** ≪限定≫ What is counted: value realized by the **project system** — the project and its successors (licensee, acquirer) — not spillovers realized by imitators. Both V-with-φ (the project system's realized share) and V-without-φ reported; headline = with-φ, per the stated objective (industrial contribution realized through this project's path). The choice is a policy-objective declaration, not a welfare claim. Cites: Brandenburger & Stuart 1996; Teece 1986.
- **P36** Displacement δ_u (domestic incumbents' lost value added) and the **no-double-deduction rule** between δ_u and α_u (a domestic competitor's alternative realization must be deducted once, not twice); rule stated, worked example in SM-D. Cites: Bloom, Schankerman & Van Reenen 2013; Myers & Lanahan 2022.
- **P37** Counterfactual schedule α_u(t), L_u: until the estimated acceleration horizon, only the acceleration wedge counts; beyond it, only the location wedge. Time-shape declared as an approximation with its direction (late-realizing scenarios overstated; monitored in calibration).
- **P38** ≪開示≫ α_u under partial identification: it is not calibratable in principle; we bound it from competitor audits and report the **breakeven α at which project rankings flip**. Disclosed: at Tier-0 defaults the deduction binds as a near-constant factor (elasticity ≈ 0) — it starts doing selection work only for projects reaching market within L_u. Cites: Manski (partial identification, 書誌確定 at S6).
- **P39** Discounting: social rate from a Ramsey decomposition (2%, sensitivity 1%/4%); risk premia excluded because failure lives in scenario probabilities and parameter ignorance lives in B_0; Arrow–Lind boundary acknowledged (idiosyncratic risk spread thin; correlated macro exposure flagged as a limit). Numeraire premise stated. Cites: Arrow & Lind 1970; Weitzman 1998; Gollier 2012; Damodaran 2009 (failure-vs-discount separation).
- **P40** Continuation value: default-rule extension + perpetuity on surviving applications under obsolescence hazard; ≪開示≫ continuation share of V is reported per project and cells above 50% are review-flagged (the least verifiable assumption dominating the number is a reportable condition, not a hidden one). ⚠数値.
- **P41** Unit-economics gate: applications enter the active set only where the willingness-to-pay cap exceeds the production-cost floor (m_u > 0); floors from process-type priors updated by evidence; the gate is a model rule, not a go/no-go verdict. Cites: Wright 1936; Lafond et al. 2018; Gutowski et al. 2009.
- **P42** Exit-form independence implemented: when self-propulsion fails, mass splits into application pivot / M&A / licensing / return-to-research (zero value; a later revival is a new project); successor completion probabilities discount the tail; license/M&A offers arrive by hazards tied to appropriability, evidence level, momentum, and carrier fill. Nine classes, one yen scale.
- **P43** Two-number decomposition ≪限定≫: acceleration wedge (a world-welfare gain) vs. location wedge (a transfer between jurisdictions) reported separately — the framework prices an industrial-policy objective and says so; it does not claim world-welfare optimality. (Beggar-thy-neighbor critique answered by construction.)

## §6 Operating the system (700w, P44–P49)

- **P44** Gates are externally verifiable events only (third-party replication, standards tests, paid PoC with materiality thresholds, production terms with signatures); splitting gates to farm passage counts is displayed as deviation and collapsed in computation; evidence grades rise by default and are demoted by explicit withdrawal or staleness (12/18/24-month rules).
- **P45** Carrier functions: eight functions, fill requires recent, multi-point, evidence-floored working records (titles and intentions do not count); vacancy delays rather than penalizes. ≪開示≫ The product form excludes compensation and threshold effects among vacancies (an assumption, stated — founding-team evidence suggests bundles matter: Beckman et al. 2007; Klotz et al. 2014); carrier supply is shared across a builder's portfolio, so per-project scores are not portfolio-consistent (scope condition, direction of bias stated). Cites: Morgeson et al. 2010; Ocasio 1997.
- **P46** Event registry: one event, one row, typed targets (state / parameter / funding windows / rules); no derived quantities as targets (single-entry bookkeeping against double counting); "thin-record" flag separates unrecorded from vacant.
- **P47** ≪開示≫ Gaming resistance, both faces: what the design holds (gate verifiability, evidence-grade demotion, deviation display, plan registration by the evaluator with second-person approval) and what it does not — the two highest-leverage inputs (ceilings P̄_u, counterfactual α_u) sit outside the calibration plan; reactive-measurement literature says that is where pressure will go. Cites: Espeland & Sauder 2007; Bevan & Hood 2006; Campbell 1979.
- **P48** Evaluator incentives: the builder is operator, evaluator, and investor; countermeasures declared as design (prior registration of B_0 before scoring, separation of estimator and investment decider, prospective registry with pre-stated falsification conditions), not as achieved fact.
- **P49** Calibration plan with identification constraints: award rate and window arrival enter only as a product (need separate observation); c's median pinned to 1.0 as a scale convention; m_n fixed at 1 until state dependence and heterogeneity separate; stop rules; ≪開示≫ the ledger currently holds 51 awards and **zero rejections** — the highest-elasticity coefficient cannot yet be calibrated from internal data. Cites: Cox 1972 (stratified-baseline form).

## §7 Application and evidence (1,500w, P50–P60)

- **P50** Sample and freeze: 21 projects on one builder's ledger; stratified into pre-incorporation projects (Before Zero proper) and incorporated projects re-scored **retrospectively at a pre-incorporation evaluation date with information restricted to that date** (projects where this restriction cannot be enforced are dropped, with count). Input freeze date and model version pinned (SM-G). ⚠数値(件数・凍結日).
- **P51** Dual-scoring design: every project scored twice — records-only (what the operational database supports) vs. records-plus-elicitation (structured interviews with the principal); same model, same version, only the input evidence differs.
- **P52** Main result: distribution of score ratios between the two runs. ⚠数値. Expectation from completed cases: ratios reach orders of magnitude.
- **P53** Representative case (composite/anonymized, p10): records-only input ranked it 2nd (≈¥103bn); four elicited facts — burn horizon, no production entry, principal's credit standing, misread addressable market — moved it to 19th (≈¥0.05bn). A ×2,000 revision from information the database did not hold. ⚠数値(匿名化はS4で再判定).
- **P54** Structural counterpart: sensitivity of the one organizational input the model accepts (evangelist-function fill e) is near-zero across its full range in two live cases — organizational collapse currently enters only through evidence-level reassignment. The observation system defines organizational observations (八機能・registry types) that the operating database had no place to receive until late 2026-08. Information insufficiency is partly **designed into** operational records.
- **P55** The finding, stated: **project rankings are dominated by information that operational records do not capture; records capture progress, not the reasons progress stopped.** Implication for any scoring practice built on "the database": the binding constraint is not the aggregation formula but the observation system.
- **P56** Ceiling sensitivity as a measurement claim: two successive re-specifications of the ceiling (total market → obtainable segment) moved individual scores by up to three orders of magnitude within days; V is constructively proportional to the ceiling (A6). Ex-ante selection output is **order-of-magnitude sensitive to how the market is sliced** — reported as a property of the object, with the disclosed caveat that 10/50/90 bands do not propagate ceiling uncertainty (A7). ⚠数値.
- **P57** Internal verification (synthetic, separated from evidence): 12 type×regulation degenerate cells; the incorporation asymmetry (pre: quality of the idea dominates, cash barely moves V; post: cash dominates — matching the fifth premise); regulated sectors realize value through licensing/M&A rather than own production, consistent with practice. ⚠数値(dormancy 承認状態に依存).
- **P58** Elasticities (funding-access coefficients rank first in every cell — calibration priority), grid convergence (report to two significant digits), and the honesty rule that "a coefficient placed" ≠ "a coefficient at work" (A1–A16 declared).
- **P59** ≪開示≫ Full nine-class probabilities including termination mass (55–81% at Tier-0 defaults); unimplemented classes shown at 0.0% (pivot; policy search π* → design only); continuation-value shares per cell.
- **P60** Verification vs. validation language (Oreskes et al. 1994): everything in §7 is implementation verification plus retrospective application; predictive validity is a registry question (§8). Known limits list (single builder; judgment-quality inputs; uncalibrated coefficients; self-propulsion value share unverified against displacement).

## §8 Policy implications and research program (700w, P61–P66)

- **P61** For policy: an objective function already denominated in domestic value added connects directly to the industrial-policy ledger (economic-security programs, mission-oriented agencies, ARPA-type institutions that do project-level ex-ante selection); exit-form independence prices licensing, M&A, and small-firm self-sufficiency on the same scale as unicorn exits — an operational alternative to unicorn-count objectives. Cites: Mazzucato 2018 (要確認 at S6); Lerner & Nanda 2020; Gaddy et al. 2017 (要確認).
- **P62** For universities/TTOs: the registry and gate table give triage a defined object (the registered plan) and an auditable evidence trail; what it costs is the observation discipline of §6.
- **P63** Timing diagnosis (early/late incorporation as plan-vs-optimal-rule gap) is designed but unimplemented; stated as the second paper's centerpiece, not smuggled here.
- **P64** Prospective registry declaration with falsification conditions: ceiling stability across versions; breakeven-α flips; dual-scoring ratio convergence after registry redesign; carrier-function interaction tests; left-truncation-corrected outcome tracking from first contact. Each condition: what observation would force which revision.
- **P65** Ongoing model revisions (organizational inputs as per-project observables; pre-incorporation cash-as-speed) — direction disclosed; frozen out of this paper's numbers by SM-G.
- **P66** External validity path: a second organization running the registry/gate table unmodified is the test of "design requirements" vs. "in-house procedure"; invitation framed as engaged scholarship. Cites: Van de Ven 2007.

## §9 Conclusion (250w, P67–P68)

- **P67** Restate: the object of evaluation for firm-less projects is a registered plan over an observable state; additionality logic then prices what the plan is expected to create for domestic industry. The apparatus exists, runs, and exposes its own weakest joints.
- **P68** The measurement problem was never the formula; it is what gets observed. Close on the registry as the research program.

## §T タイトル3案（まさ判断待ち）

いずれも「最初」を主張せず、additionality の語彙と Before Zero の名前の立て方で差を付けた。

- **(a) 名前を旗艦に**: *The Before Zero Model: additionality-based valuation of university deep-tech projects before the firm exists*
  — BZM の名前で prior art を立てる（目的2=学術体系構築に直結）。旧タイトル (b) の系譜。
- **(b) 転換を旗艦に**: *Valuing plans, not ventures: an additionality-based observation system for deep-tech projects before incorporation*
  — N2（評価対象の転換）を題名にする。査読者への新規性の見せ方は最も鋭いが、BZM の名前が消える。
- **(c) 領域を旗艦に**: *Before the firm exists: measuring the industrial value of university deep-tech projects at the pre-company stage*
  — 領域宣言を最前面に。名前も転換も副題以下へ。JOTT の実務読者には最も分かりやすい。

**えいみの推奨は (a)**。理由: 目的2（Before Zero 領域の学術体系構築）には固有名の確立が効き、旧タイトル判断（まさ 2026-07-03「bがいい!」= BZM 名を立てる案）とも整合する。(b) の「plans, not ventures」は §1 の一撃文と abstract に温存できるので、題名で消費しなくても失われない。
