## Rewrite intent

The full-book ruthless audit identified Ch16-Ch18 as the largest theory-drag zone:

- Ch16 repeated `あとでTRL/BRL/GRL/SRL/HRLと呼ぶ` in a glossary rhythm.
- Ch17 interrupted the public-grant corridor with `mu_A/mu_G/mu_I/sigma_SU` staging.
- Ch18 repeated Ch13's founder-candidate scene and over-explained FRL / F_character / F_capability.

This pass demoted theory-name staging while preserving the underlying model concepts.

## Changes

### Ch16

Rebuilt the chapter around a single sentence: `準備はできている`.

Instead of introducing readiness axes one by one, the meeting rewrites the sentence for five recipients:

- researcher / technology;
- company / business and budget owner;
- university / disclosure and governance;
- hospital / social acceptance and operational burden;
- young commercialization talent / organization and responsibility.

The theory names now appear once near the end as names for a split the scene has already forced.

### Ch17

Kept the 8:12 public-grant email and same-week clock pressure.

Demoted `mu_A`, `mu_G`, `mu_I`, and repeated `sigma_SU` staging. The main artifact is now the regional corridor drawn on the whiteboard: university, hospital, company, municipality, grant, disclosure, and budget-owner stops.

`sigma_SU` appears once, after the reader has already seen the corridor mismatch.

### Ch18

Compressed the chapter into a placement-memo chapter rather than another external-candidate test.

The chapter now asks:

- who carries what;
- what breaks if a function is assigned to the wrong person;
- what can be supplemented from outside;
- what cannot be delegated by title alone.

FRL / F_character / F_capability appear in one late naming block, after the role-placement memo has done the work.
