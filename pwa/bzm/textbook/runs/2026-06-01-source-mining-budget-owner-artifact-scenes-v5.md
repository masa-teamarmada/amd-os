### Public / Internal Classification Rules

#### public_ready

Can enter public manuscript after light prose editing and anonymization:

- A company says "interesting" but no budget owner, evaluator, procurement path, or field operator appears.
- A WAIT decision has named owners, return conditions, and a review date.
- A slide is color-coded into public / limited / do-not-disclose-yet zones.
- A young commercialization person earns authority by saying "未確認です" at the right moment.
- unknown and not_started are separated before judging an institution.
- RESOURCE_SHIFT sends effort from investor meetings toward the lowest readiness axis.

#### public_rewrite

High-value, but must be composited, anonymized, and stripped of internal language:

- VC due diligence taking months before incorporation.
- GAP-style pitch support followed by external CEO pressure.
- Researcher "life direction" and research respect as trust input.
- External executive fit/misfit scenes.
- Institution pilot gates based on ERS / policy matrix logic.
- Field-note safety loop and L2 Textbook insight process as a method, not implementation.

#### internal_only

Do not use in public manuscript body:

- Company names, event names, project names, individual names, thread/worker/司令塔 language.
- Raw transcript text, source paths, branch names, DB table rows, source hashes, migration IDs, service-role/API/applier details.
- Patent/legal conclusions, prices, contracts, revenue assumptions, named institution evaluations.
- Person-level FRL/HRL/ALQ/Grit/Resilience assessments.
- Score weights, exact formulas, thresholds, calibration, or implementation commands.

## 2. Composite Case Bank

### Composite Case 01: The Interesting Company With No Budget Owner

- `status`: public_ready
- `chapters`: 01, 09, 16, 19, 20, 23, 24
- `scene`: A technical scout says the technology is interesting and asks for a deck. The slide later says "customer candidate," but nobody has met the person who owns the budget, evaluation criteria, field site, procurement risk, or failure consequence.
- `tension`: The team wants to count the company as customer validation because everyone needs momentum.
- `usable point`: Corporate interest is a signal. Customer truth begins when a problem owner, evaluation condition, budget route, and next action appear.
- `artifact`: customer signal ladder row, "interesting" email, meeting memo, budget-owner blank cell.
- `public route`: Keep the company anonymous and sector-general. Use roles: scout, evaluator, budget owner, field operator, procurement/legal.

### Composite Case 02: The Slide That Outruns Protection

- `status`: public_ready
- `chapters`: 00, 02, 05, 09, 16, 20, 23
- `scene`: One slide begins as an internal explanation, then appears in a company deck, grant deck, investor summary, and follow-up email before disclosure categories are agreed.
- `tension`: No one is malicious. Everyone is trying to move. The artifact moves faster than consent, IP review, and researcher trust.
- `usable point`: Disclosure design controls the speed of misunderstanding.
- `artifact`: red/yellow/blue slide map plus distribution log.
- `public route`: Do not include technology content. Make the slide memorable through its movement and boundary status.

### Composite Case 03: WAIT With Work, Then GO

- `status`: public_rewrite
- `chapters`: 06, 07, 11, 12, 19, 20, 23
- `scene`: The team writes WAIT on the board, then assigns four owners for 90 days: budget-owner confirmation, disclosure map, field-site condition, founder-function gap.
- `tension`: WAIT looks like lost momentum to the grant room and investor room.
- `usable point`: WAIT becomes legitimate only when it has owners, return conditions, and a date.
- `artifact`: WAIT decision record, 90-day review memo, before/after evidence table.
- `public route`: Use a composite outcome. Do not imply a specific real case became GO unless publishable.

### Composite Case 04: RESOURCE_SHIFT Before The Pitch Becomes Too Good

- `status`: public_ready after composite
- `chapters`: 06, 07, 11, 12, 19, 23
- `scene`: The pitch deck improves weekly, but the lowest readiness axis remains business evidence. The decision is not "more pitch practice"; it is RESOURCE_SHIFT to customer conditions and field constraints.
- `tension`: Polished storytelling competes with weak evidence.
- `usable point`: Activity volume is not progress unless resources move toward the bottleneck.
- `artifact`: integrated low-axis table, "what we will not do this month" memo.
- `public route`: Generalize from pitch/deck pressure, no program names.

### Composite Case 05: The Young Operator's First 30 Days

- `status`: public_ready
- `chapters`: 08, 10, 18, 21, 24
- `scene`: A junior commercialization person starts without authority. They earn trust by reading the paper, separating fact/hypothesis/unconfirmed, tracking deck distribution, and returning company feedback without exaggeration.
- `tension`: The same person can also lose trust if they over-translate the research into market language.
- `usable point`: Before the title comes process memory and boundary discipline.
- `artifact`: Day 1 / Day 3 / Day 10 / Day 20 / Day 30 log.
- `public route`: Avoid age, school, employer, and gender markers. Make it a role arc.

### Composite Case 06: Strong Resume, Weak Complementability

- `status`: public_rewrite
- `chapters`: 08, 13, 18, 20, 23
- `scene`: An external executive candidate has impressive experience but repeatedly smooths over research constraints in investor language. The issue is not whether the person is "bad"; it is whether the missing function is actually complemented.
- `tension`: The room wants a strong CV to solve anxiety about CEO function.
- `usable point`: External CEO fit is complementability across trust, technical respect, role clarity, and decision cadence.
- `artifact`: complementability interview sheet, protected-line repeat-back test.
- `public route`: Never include identifiable career details. Contrast functions, not personality.

### Composite Case 07: Authentic Researcher, Missing Operating Function

- `status`: public_ready after composite
- `chapters`: 04, 08, 13, 18, 19
- `scene`: The researcher is trusted, technically clear, and socially meaningful, but cannot carry weekly commercial operations alone.
- `tension`: The room mistakes authenticity for full CEO capability, then overcorrects toward replacing the researcher.
- `usable point`: Researcher authenticity can remain central while operating capability is complemented.
- `artifact`: founder-function split table.
- `public route`: Frame as "which function is missing" rather than "who is suitable."

### Composite Case 08: Institution Pilot Gate Before Campus-Wide Rollout

- `status`: public_rewrite
- `chapters`: 14, 21, 24
- `scene`: A research institution wants a support system for all seeds. The safer first step is a narrow pilot: three seeds, clear intake, disclosure review, customer-signal ladder, monthly low-axis review, and owner map.
- `tension`: Ambition wants a full program. Trust requires a small gate.
- `usable point`: Institution readiness begins with a responsibility pipeline, not a support-menu count.
- `artifact`: pilot charter, intake criteria, stop/expand gates.
- `public route`: Remove institution name, contract scope, pricing, access design.

### Composite Case 09: Unknown Misread As Not Started

- `status`: public_ready
- `chapters`: 14, 21, 24
- `scene`: A meeting labels the institution weak because a policy or route is not visible in the first conversation. Later, a staff member finds an existing but unused process.
- `tension`: Mislabeling unknown as absence destroys trust. Calling real absence unknown hides work.
- `usable point`: unknown is a verification task. not_started is a build task.
- `artifact`: policy status matrix with `unknown`, `not_started`, `drafting`, `established`.
- `public route`: Do not include actual institution assessments.

### Composite Case 10: Field Notes Become Safer Questions

- `status`: internal_only source, public_rewrite method
- `chapters`: 10, 20, 22, 23
- `scene`: A private meeting note contains a useful pattern: a supportive company contact had no budget route. The publishable form is not the story. It is the reusable question: "Which role did the positive contact actually hold?"
- `tension`: The book needs vivid scenes, but not at the cost of source leakage.
- `usable point`: Learning is stored as reusable questions and artifact patterns, not raw private stories.
- `artifact`: field-note safety loop.
- `public route`: Never mention local applier, DB, candidate, source hash, path, or raw transcript.
