## Order Plan

This pass rewrites the opening arc as one continuous reader experience:

1. Prologue: open with a composite field contradiction before explaining Before Zero.
2. Chapter 1: show false acceleration from research result to apparent startup progress.
3. Chapter 2: make the same milestone collide across actor clocks.
4. Chapter 3: make support isolation the emotional peak of Part 1.
5. Chapter 4: promote the GAP fund / VC / CEO-function contradiction into the central hinge.
6. Chapter 5: move disclosure after the hinge as the next risk of outward acceleration.
7. Chapter 6: tie incorporation timing to unresolved functions, disclosure, fundraising/DD duration, and irreversible expectations.

The public body should stay company-neutral and reader-first. Formal theory is
only a bridge for later chapters, not the explanation engine in this opening arc.

## Self Critique

### Still Weak

- The chapters now open with sharper field moments, but the composite case bank
  is still thin. More varied scenes beyond pitch / investor / disclosure rooms
  are needed so the book does not over-index on the GAP fund / VC / CEO
  contradiction.
- Chapters 2 and 3 can still become more sensory and specific: who is sitting in
  the room, what is written in the follow-up mail, what the researcher cannot
  say aloud.
- Chapter 6 needs more lived detail around the first months after incorporation:
  bank account, accounting, license negotiation, conflict-of-interest review,
  and the founder's calendar becoming fragmented.
