## Executive summary

Ch11-Ch14 already carry the correct middle-book concepts: macro tailwinds,
readiness axes, founder function, and institution-as-nursery. The drag is that
the chapters often explain the right distinction after a scene instead of making
a new consequence happen inside the scene.

The next rewrite should not add more theory. It should add one visible near
accident or cost per chapter:

- Ch11: a public grant / policy / investor heat moment almost creates a dangerous
  sentence in an application, email, or investor update.
- Ch12: a single `準備できています` answer almost sends the team into the wrong
  next step before the readiness axes are split.
- Ch13: external founder-function friction should move beyond the existing
  second interview scene by testing how strong language, weak facts, and the
  researcher's protected line behave under a different pressure.
- Ch14: support menus should produce a concrete next-researcher loneliness scene,
  then the responsibility pipeline should change that scene.

Recommended editorial move: add consequence, then compress explanation. If a new
paper or memo appears, it must change the next room, not become another list.

## Why Ch11-Ch14 currently drag

The full-book ruthless audit placed Ch11-Ch14 at 6.9/10 for page-turner pull.
The diagnosis is consistent across the prior Act IV cold read and the later
full-book audit:

- Ch11 has a strong three-email opening, but the tailwind is then mostly
  interpreted. The reader needs to see the heat change a document.
- Ch12 has the right sentence, `準備できていますか`, but the chapter becomes
  diagnostic. The reader needs one near-wrong decision caused by answering in
  the singular.
- Ch13 has a strong external-candidate scene, so adding another similar interview
  would feel repetitive. The useful new material is behavior under bad facts,
  title pressure, 90-day responsibility, or research-boundary repair.
- Ch14 has the right institutional thesis, but it risks policy memo tone. The
  chapter needs one next researcher who still gets lost despite a rich menu, and
  one small pipeline change that reduces the burden.

The shared drag pattern: the manuscript often shows a paper and then explains
the paper. The next pass should show a paper causing someone to stop, apologize,
rewrite, delay, cut activity, or take responsibility.

## Public/internal classification

Use this classification before any rewrite:

- `public_ready`: Can be used as a public composite scene after ordinary
  anonymization and style work. It still should not include source paths, raw
  notes, private URLs, exact quotes, names, dates, institutions, or amounts.
- `public_rewrite`: High-value source, but it needs substantial translation
  before use. Convert company-subject language, internal model terms, exact
  source mechanics, and project-specific proof into reader-owned field behavior.
- `internal_only`: Use only as editorial evidence or method source. Do not paste
  into public manuscript. This includes raw transcript wording, DB schemas,
  source hashes, exact project/person/institution data, finance amounts,
  internal workflow labels, local applier mechanics, and private relation
  assessments.

Chapter use recommendation:

| chapter | primary insertions | avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Ch11 | C01, C03, C04, C06 or C08 | More macro explanation; raw policy specifics. |
| Ch12 | C01, C03, C04, C06, C08 | Full TRL/BRL/GRL/SRL/HRL teaching block before consequence. |
| Ch13 | C01, C02, C03, C05, C06 | Repeating the same second-interview scene already in the body. |
| Ch14 | C01, C02, C03, C04, C08 | Another support-menu inventory without a before/after researcher. |

Internal-only red lines:

- Do not quote raw transcript or raw DB.
- Do not identify actual projects, institutions, individuals, private URLs, or
  exact commercial terms.
- Do not publish internal words such as source paths, commander/worker language,
  local applier, candidate IDs, route names, exact scoring values, or approval
  states.
- Do not turn support actors into villains. The conflict should come from
  locally correct pressures stacking without a responsibility pipeline.
