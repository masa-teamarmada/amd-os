The page-turner engine is now credible because it is artifact-driven rather than
concept-driven. A cold reader can remember: the dangerous one-line customer
claim, the slide that almost travels too far, WAIT with owners, a later
RESOURCE_SHIFT, and the old/new evidence rule.

What still slows the book:

- Repeated chapter-end machinery: `今日の行動、まだ残る不安`,
  `会議を閉じる前に残すもの`, `次章へ残す未解決`.
- Explicit curriculum language: `後半の理論への橋` appears in Ch11, Ch12, Ch13,
  and Ch14.
- Several strong scenes resolve too quickly into explanation or tables.
- Ch22-24 are useful, but their first lines tell the reader they are no longer
  in the same narrative mode.

Best next move: keep the current spine, then vary rhythm. One chapter should end
with a changed relationship, one with a revised artifact, one with a still-open
conflict, and one with a concrete decision rather than another table.
