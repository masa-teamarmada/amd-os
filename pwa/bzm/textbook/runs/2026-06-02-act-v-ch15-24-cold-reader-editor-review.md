# 2026-06-02 Act V Ch15-24 cold-reader/editor review

## Executive verdict

Act V is now readable as a continuation of the main composite case, but it is not yet publication-grade.

The rewrite at `139d862` successfully removed most markdown-table / checklist dump behavior from Ch19-Ch24. Ch19 is the strongest new chapter: survival probability, earning body, small paid evidence, J-curve / IPO pressure, and RESOURCE_SHIFT now appear as a live decision rather than an abstract principle.

However, the back third still has two editorial problems:

1. Ch15-Ch18 still leaks theory names too early in several places. The scene often earns the concept, but the prose then announces the concept in a classroom-like way.
2. Ch22-Ch24 still announce themselves as Toolkit A/B/C. They work better as a separate Field Toolkit appendix than as the emotional ending of the book.

Recommended next move: do not full-rewrite the whole manuscript. Instead run a surgical residue pass plus appendix strategy pass.

## Act V as book ending

Ch15-Ch21 can serve as the ending of the main narrative arc.

The strongest ending path is:

- Ch15: the case has become too tangled for field language alone.
- Ch16: readiness splits into different kinds of readiness.
- Ch17: macro heat and local PoC clocks do not align.
- Ch18: founder readiness is not personality judgment or CEO replacement.
- Ch19: high expectation does not equal GO; survival probability and earning body change the decision.
- Ch20: when the map fails, evidence rules are revised instead of assigning blame.
- Ch21: the burden returns to the institution; the nursery must be designed.

This gives the book a coherent closing movement: the deck sentence that became too strong eventually becomes a set of institutional responsibilities.

Ch22-Ch24 are useful, but they currently feel like after-matter. That is not bad. It only means they should be framed as Field Toolkit rather than pretending to be the final emotional chapters.

- `pwa/bzm/public-manuscript/16-readiness-axes-field-guide.md`: repeated `あとで...と呼ぶ` lines make the chapter feel like a lecture. Replace with one short introductory sentence after the whole scene has split, then let the labels appear as marginal names.
- `pwa/bzm/public-manuscript/18-founder-readiness-field-first.md`: the FRL / F_character / F_capability block is still definition-heavy. Keep the repeat-back test and 90-day role negotiation, then compress the definitions into one paragraph.
