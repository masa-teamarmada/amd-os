## Chapter-Level Intent

### 15 Why Model The Field

- Created `pwa/bzm/public-manuscript/15-why-model-the-field.md`.
- Opens from a support-room scene where everyone says something true, but
  different unresolved blanks remain: customer, founder function, disclosure,
  and first six months after incorporation.
- Reframes modeling as a map for reducing misreadings, not a tool for forcing
  the field into a theory.
- Includes filled tables for field language, misread risk, and GO / WAIT / HOLD
  / NO_GO / RESOURCE_SHIFT.
- Bridge: field signals become TRL, BRL, GRL, SRL, HRL, FRL, ERS, and integrated
  judgment only after the reader has seen why the distinctions matter.

### 16 Readiness Axes Field Guide

- Created `pwa/bzm/public-manuscript/16-readiness-axes-field-guide.md`.
- Opens from a meeting where everyone says "ready" while meaning different
  things.
- Explains TRL / BRL / GRL / SRL / HRL from field confusion: research proof,
  customer proof, disclosure order, social acceptance, and role/function
  readiness.
- Includes filled customer-signal translation table.
- Bridge: XRL is a later name for field distinctions, not a first-principles
  vocabulary dump.

### 17 Macro Alignment And Triple Helix

- Created `pwa/bzm/public-manuscript/17-macro-alignment-and-triple-helix.md`.
- Opens from a research cluster with strong seeds, policy money, and nearby
  industry, but no automatic company formation.
- Explains sigma_SU through phase gaps among research, industry, policy,
  capital, and social acceptance.
- Includes filled tables mapping mu_A / mu_I / mu_G to field waves and common
  misreadings.
- Bridge: sigma_SU is not mood or expectation; it is a way to read which wave is
  early, which wave is late, and which work follows.

### 18 Founder Readiness Field First

- Created `pwa/bzm/public-manuscript/18-founder-readiness-field-first.md`.
- Opens from the tension between a researcher whose authenticity moves people
  and an external executive candidate whose resume is strong but trust fit is
  weak.
- Splits founder readiness into three field categories required by commander
  feedback: non-delegable traits, complementable execution, and external
  executive fit.
- Includes a filled contrasting-candidate table.
- Bridge: FRL / F_character / F_capability are used to prevent crude
  personality judgment and to show what can or cannot be complemented.

### 19 Integrated Score As Next Action

- Created `pwa/bzm/public-manuscript/19-integrated-score-as-next-action.md`.
- Opens from a high-potential case where the next action is still unclear
  because customer validation, disclosure, founder function, and university
  procedure are unresolved.
- Frames integrated score as next-action support, not ranking or grading.
- Includes filled bottleneck / next-action table and decision-branch table.
- Bridge: threshold language is treated as a conversation starter and
  uncertainty reducer, not an automatic gate.

### 20 Retrofit Validation As Learning

- Created `pwa/bzm/public-manuscript/20-retrofit-validation-as-learning.md`.
- Opens after investor feedback lands emotionally as personal failure.
- Reframes retrofit and validation as learning from past judgment maps rather
  than assigning blame.
- Includes filled reflection table and composite timeline table.
- Bridge: model misses become evidence for revising the model, not a reason to
  defend the model.

### 21 Institution Readiness As Nursery

- Created `pwa/bzm/public-manuscript/21-institution-readiness-as-nursery.md`.
- Opens from a strong individual seed where the pre-company burden still flows
  back to the researcher because institutional pipeline is fragmented.
- Frames ERS as responsibility pipeline and nursery readiness, not support menu
  count or institution ranking.
- Includes filled menu-vs-nursery, unknown-vs-not_started, and nursery
  self-check tables.
- Bridge: ERS is separate from individual venture readiness and should not be
  mixed into the individual score.
