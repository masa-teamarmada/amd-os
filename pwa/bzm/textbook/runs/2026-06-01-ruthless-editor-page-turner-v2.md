## 0. Ruthless Verdict

The manuscript has moved from "internal explanation" to "reader-first practical
book skeleton." That is real progress. It is no longer obviously a company
brochure, and the latest branch versions add scenes, filled tables, and better
chapter-end pulls.

But it is still not yet a page-turner.

The central weakness is that the manuscript now has many good field lenses, but
not enough narrative obligation. The reader learns useful things in almost
every chapter, yet rarely feels that the next chapter is emotionally required.
The book gives competent relief too quickly. It often says: "Here is the trap,
here is the table, here is what to ask." A sellable book needs: "I know this
room. This contradiction hurts. I need the next page to explain why reasonable
people keep producing this damage."

The next rewrite should not merely add more examples. It must install a
recurring multi-case spine, keep one wound open per chapter, and make each tool
arrive as a response to pressure the reader already felt.

Current editorial grade: **B- as a practical manuscript skeleton, C as an
addictive commercial reading experience.**

## 3. Narrative Spine v2

GAP/VC/CEO is the best hinge, but it cannot be the only engine. The book should
run five recurring case threads that cross, diverge, and return.

### Thread A: Researcher Role Whiplash

Opening wound: the researcher is trained to stand forward, then questioned as
the person who should not run the company.

Crosses:

- Ch00: first contradiction.
- Ch03: support rooms multiply.
- Ch04: CEO function is decomposed.
- Ch08: responsibility map assigns what the researcher carries.
- Ch13: founder readiness separates non-delegable authenticity from
  complementable execution.

Reader pull: researchers feel "this is why I feel confused"; URA and investors
feel "this is how our reasonable demands land on one person."

### Thread B: The Story Travels Faster Than Protection

Opening wound: pitch materials, investor materials, company materials, and
unpublished research start to share the same images and claims.

Crosses:

- Ch01: false acceleration from polished deck.
- Ch02: clocks collide around one document deadline.
- Ch05: disclosure layers and consent.
- Ch09: investor materials split fact / hypothesis / unverified.
- Ch12: GRL becomes one readiness axis.

Reader pull:産連/URA and TLO readers feel seen; researchers feel protected;
investors learn why "just send the deck" can be dangerous.

### Thread C: Corporate Interest That Does Not Yet Become Customer Truth

Opening wound: a company says "interesting," and everyone quietly upgrades it
into customer validation.

Crosses:

- Ch01: `面白い` is not validation.
- Ch02: company clock is different from support-program clock.
- Ch08: who owns customer learning.
- Ch09: customer signal ladder before risk capital.
- Ch10: failed company interview becomes learning log.
- Ch12: BRL is named after the reader has already seen the error.

Reader pull: venture builders and corporate readers see the trap of polite
interest; investors see a cleaner way to ask for evidence.

### Thread D: WAIT As Work

Opening wound: everyone wants motion, but the right decision is not yet GO.

Crosses:

- Ch06: incorporation creates irreversible obligations.
- Ch07: decision meeting writes WAIT with return condition.
- Ch09: capital is framed as uncertainty reduction.
- Ch10: failed attempts update the return condition.
- Ch11: macro heat does not override missing conditions.
- Ch12: lowest axis determines what WAIT must work on.

Reader pull: institutional staff gain language to slow a process without
appearing passive; investors see that "too early" can become a work plan.

### Thread E: Nursery Without Responsibility Pipeline

Opening wound: support menus exist, but nobody owns the path from seed to
company-readiness.

Crosses:

- Ch03: support increases loneliness.
- Ch07: decision log needs an owner.
- Ch08: responsibility vacancies appear.
- Ch10: supporter's wrong hypotheses are logged.
- Ch14: institution as nursery explains the source layer.
- Theory: ERS names the nursery, not the individual venture.

Reader pull: university leadership, public research institutes, regional
ecosystems, and policy readers feel "this is about our system, not only one
case."

## 4. Reader-Specific Reading Lines

### Researcher

They should enter through vulnerability: "I am not slow; I am being asked to
hold unintegrated expectations." The book must then give permission to separate
what only the researcher can carry from what others must carry.

Required emotional path: relief -> anger at crude role framing -> practical
agency -> trust in structured WAIT/role maps.

### Industry-Collaboration / URA / TLO

They should enter through operational pressure: "I am the only person seeing
all clocks, but everyone else wants their own deadline satisfied." The book must
give them artifacts they can put into meetings.

Required emotional path: recognition -> permission to slow sequence -> tools
for disclosure / decision / responsibility -> institution-pipeline redesign.

### Young Venture Builder / CEO / EIR Candidate

They should enter through invitation: "I can be useful before I have the CEO
title." The book must show a humble path to carrying a function without
capturing the researcher's center.

Required emotional path: aspiration -> restraint -> first 30 days of useful
work -> readiness to earn trust before authority.

### Investor / CVC / Business Company

They should enter through the mirror: "My reasonable question may be heard as
identity judgment if the support system has not decomposed the missing
function." The book must not flatter them; it should teach better diagnostics.

Required emotional path: discomfort -> better translation of feedback ->
clearer evidence requests -> respect for pre-company sequencing.

### Institution / Policy / Ecosystem Builder

They should enter through responsibility: "More programs do not automatically
make a nursery." The book must show how support menus become a pipeline.

Required emotional path: pride in existing supports -> unease about gaps ->
pipeline view -> ERS as a design lens, not ranking.

## 6. Rewrite Gate

The next public manuscript rewrite **fails** unless all conditions below are
met.

### Scene / Artifact Gate

- Every chapter has at least one concrete scene or document artifact.
- At least five chapters include a filled tool using the same recurring case
  thread.
- No chapter may open with a definition unless a prior scene has already made
  the definition necessary.

### Tension Gate

- Every chapter ending must contain:
  - one action the reader can take;
  - one unresolved question;
  - one reason the next chapter is necessary.
- A chapter ending fails if it could be replaced by "まとめると".
- At least three chapters must leave emotional pressure unresolved, not only
  intellectual curiosity.

### Recurring Case Gate

- At least three recurring case threads must span two or more chapters.
- At least one thread must span five or more chapters.
- GAP/VC/CEO may be the hinge, but not the only spine.
- Ch14 must explicitly return to Ch03's researcher loneliness.

### Specificity Gate

- General paragraphs without scene, artifact, named actor role, or decision
  consequence are forbidden unless they are shorter than 120 Japanese
  characters and bridge between concrete beats.
- Lists of questions must be replaced or preceded by filled examples.
- Tools must show sample entries, not empty frameworks.

### Redundancy Gate

- Ch04 owns CEO-function contradiction.
- Ch08 owns actual responsibility placement.
- Ch13 owns founder-readiness fit and complementability.
- Ch06 owns incorporation timing pressure.
- Ch07 owns first-conversation / decision-log operation.
- If a paragraph explains the same concept in two chapters, one version must be
  cut or assigned a different job.

### Reader Pathway Gate

- Researcher, URA/TLO, young venture builder, and investor/company reader must
  each receive at least one direct "this is your next move" pathway by Ch10.
- Institution/policy reader must receive their pathway by Ch14.

### Public Safety Gate

- Public body copy must contain no internal operation terms, source paths,
  worker/thread/commander language, named private events, or company-promotion
  framing.
- Case scenes must be composite, anonymized, and public-safe.
